# Decipher v1.2.0 — Release Notes

*Released: 2026-05-02*

This is our biggest update since launch — focused on speed, finding the right book, and making it easier to get help when something breaks. If you've been waiting on a reason to come back to Decipher, this is it.

---

## What's new

### A whole new way to find books — Curated for Learners

Browsing 70,000+ books is exciting, but if you're learning Spanish from scratch, you don't need *Don Quijote* on day one. We hand-picked 44 books across 7 languages and tagged each one with a difficulty level (1 Beginner → 5 Advanced). Filter by level, see why each book works for learners, and pick something that'll actually move the needle for you.

Every curated book includes a short note explaining why it works for language learners — written by someone who's been there.

Languages with curated picks: Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Dutch.

### Sort books your way

You can now sort the Gutenberg library by **Popular**, **Newest**, or **Oldest**. Look for the sort pills in the top right of the browse screen.

### Premium upgrade is here

The free tier is still genuinely usable — 30 word lookups per day, 30 saved vocab words, 3 page-reads per day. But if you're reading every day, you'll want unlimited access. We added a one-time **$6.99 Premium upgrade** that removes all daily limits forever. No subscription, no recurring charges.

If you've been on a previous version with a promo code, your premium status carries over — you don't need to do anything.

### Faster everything

We rebuilt the way book covers and metadata load. Behind the scenes:
- Book covers now cache aggressively to your device — second visits are instant
- Library data is cached at the edge globally, so you're never waiting on a slow upstream server
- We pre-fetch the next page while you browse, so pagination feels instant
- The browse screen no longer freezes when you switch languages — you'll see a clear loading indicator

### Bug reports and feedback in the app

Found a bug? Got an idea? **Settings → Support → Report a bug** (or Send feedback) opens a form right in the app. Tell us what happened, optionally drop your email, and submit. No more bouncing out to your email client.

### A cleaner reader

Several quality-of-life improvements to the reading experience:
- **Larger header icons** with bigger touch targets — the lock, chapters, TTS, vocab, and settings icons are all easier to tap
- **Flashcards now have their own icon** in the reader header — direct access without going through Vocabulary first
- **Vocabulary, Chapters, and Settings are now full-screen** instead of partial overlays — no more peeking at the book behind a half-open panel

### Fixes

- The book detail popup wasn't opening on some screens. Now it does.
- Curated book covers were sizing too large when only a few books matched a difficulty filter.
- The download button in the book details modal sometimes got cut off on smaller phones — now it stays pinned to the bottom regardless of content length.
- Clearer empty-state copy on the Vocabulary and Flashcards screens — explains that you save a word by tapping it AND picking a highlight color (not just tapping).

---

## What's next

Working on **PDF support** for v1.3.0 — tap-to-translate and side-by-side translation for any DRM-free PDF. Great for foreign-language documents, contracts, manuals, and anything else that doesn't come as an EPUB.

If you have ideas, send them via the new in-app feedback form. We read every one.
